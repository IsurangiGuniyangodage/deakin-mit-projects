# SIT740: Research and Development in IT

**Unit:** Research and Development in Information Technology, Deakin University
**Type:** Individual research, writing and pitch work

> **Note:** This folder contains a write-up only. In line with Deakin University's academic integrity rules, I have not published assessment code or solutions. I'm happy to walk through the work in an interview.

## Overview
Skills for turning an idea into a researched, clearly communicated proposal.

## Work
- **Research methods and proposals:** reflecting on IT around me, comparing research methods, and drafting a proposal.
- **Critical reading:** analysed a paper on the energy use and life-cycle emissions of delivery drones. I revised my first draft after feedback to explain the authors' reasons, methods and models more clearly.
- **Literature review:** a mini review that I revised to connect the sources and support each claim with citations.
- **IoT data analytics:** how data analytics is used in the Internet of Things.
- **Confidentiality:** I drafted a non-disclosure agreement and considered why confidentiality matters in research collaboration.
- **PREV2X pitch:** created the pitch materials and a **concept demonstrator**, and recorded a video explaining the idea.
- **Recommendation systems:** reviewed options for an online retailer. The data was views, cart additions and purchases, so I treated them as signs of interest rather than ratings, and compared the options on practical concerns.

## Skills shown
Critical reading, structured writing, evidence-based recommendation, and presenting technical ideas to non-experts.
