# SIT726: ZenTra, an AI Joint-Budgeting App for Couples

**Unit:** IT Innovations and Entrepreneurship, Deakin University
**Role:** CTO / CDO of Team 12, leading the technical and visual design stream

> **Note:** This folder contains a write-up only. In line with Deakin University's academic integrity rules, I have not published assessment code or solutions. I'm happy to walk through the work in an interview.

## Overview
ZenTra is a concept for an AI-based joint-budgeting app for couples in Victoria, aimed at the cost-of-living pressure many households face. The unit took the idea from problem discovery to an investor-style pitch.

## What we built
- **Problem discovery:** design thinking, empathy mapping and agile iteration to define the user problem.
- **Product concept:** an AI chatbot, gamification and SMS parsing for shared budgeting.
- **Design:** wireframes and **Figma prototypes**, iterated through planning and testing so user pain points shaped the solution.
- **Business materials:** a business plan with financial models and go-to-market plans, a **Startmate accelerator application letter**, a pitch deck, and a **pitch video**.
- **Global view:** I assessed expansion routes in Australia, New Zealand, the UK and Canada, and considered AI ethics, data privacy and Open Banking standards.

## My role
As CTO/CDO I coordinated the technical and visual design stream with Figma, Google Drive and weekly sprint meetings. I kept brand consistency, technical feasibility and UX aligned, and set up a respectful feedback culture in the team.

## Skills shown
Product thinking, UX prototyping, technical leadership, investor communication and ethical reasoning about AI.

**Tools:** Figma, Google Drive. Concept and prototype stage, not a deployed product.
